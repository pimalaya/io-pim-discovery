# pimalaya-stream 0.4

The pimalaya-stream requirement moves from 0.3.1 to 0.4 and the io-http one from 0.7 to 0.8, both released on 2026-11-13, and `cargo update` refreshed the lockfile, which holds pimalaya-stream 0.4.0 and io-http 0.8.0 alone. The optional pimalaya-cli of the `cli` feature stays on 0.3 (0.3.2 after the update): its move to 0.4 is a change of its own.

The break is in the `Tls` this crate takes: pimalaya-stream 0.4's `tls::Tls` moved the trusted certificate from `cert` to `server`, a `TlsServer { cert }`, and gained `client`, the certificate presented for mutual TLS. Callers handing a `Tls` to the stream pool or the discovery clients must move to 0.4 with it.

The `pim-discovery` binary follows in src/main.rs: `From<TlsFlags> for Tls` fills `server` from `--tls-cert` and leaves `client` empty. The flag keeps its name and meaning. No doc comment or example named `Tls::cert`.

Capabilities moved: none. The stream pool, its factories and its per-endpoint reuse are unchanged.

Verified: `cargo tree -i pimalaya-stream --all-features` and `cargo tree -i io-http --all-features` each show one version, `cargo test --all-features` passed (74), `cargo clippy --all-features --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean, `cargo build --no-default-features` clean, `cargo check --no-default-features --all-targets` clean for `client`, `stream`, `cli`, `rustls-aws`, `native-tls` and `native-tls,vendored`.
